The person behind it

Reuben Onyango is a Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant and a member in good standing with the College of Immigration & Citizenship Consultants (CICC).

That licence is not a formality. It is what allows someone to represent you to Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada for a fee, it carries professional obligations that are enforceable, and it can be checked by anyone in under a minute. It is the first thing you should confirm about any person you are considering, here or anywhere else.

Why licensing matters

Only three kinds of people may represent you.

This is set out in law, and it is the clearest line you can use to protect yourself.

  1. 01

    Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant

    Licensed by the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants and bound by its Code of Professional Conduct. Registration R707677.

  2. 02

    Lawyer or Quebec notary

    Members of a Canadian provincial or territorial law society, or of the Chambre des notaires du Québec.

  3. 03

    Nobody else

    Anyone outside those categories charging a fee to advise on or prepare your application is doing so unlawfully, and is accountable to no regulator.

The College keeps a public register of every licensed consultant in Canada. Searching a name or a registration number there is free, takes seconds, and is the single most useful thing you can do before handing anyone your documents.
